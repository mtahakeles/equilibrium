How it works

Encoding

Each bill's payment week is a one-hot choice among its allowed weeks, which is how the problem would be written as a QUBO for a quantum annealer.

Energy

The cost function blends a linear term (discounts captured, penalties incurred) with a quadratic term penalizing any week the projected balance dips under the safety buffer.

Search

Simulated annealing searches it on a normal CPU: it accepts worse moves early on and gets stricter as the temperature cools.

Result

The lowest-energy schedule found becomes the recommendation: the payment order and timing least likely to put the business at risk.
